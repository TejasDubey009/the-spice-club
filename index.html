The Ambiance

Dine Beside the Venetian Canals of Doha

Located in the serene pastel waterways of Qanat Quartier—often celebrated as the Venice of Doha—The Spice Club offers an enchanting dual ambiance. Choose between our romantic outdoor canal-side terrace overlooking Venetian-style arched bridges or our intimate indoor dining hall wrapped in rich mahogany, glowing chandeliers, and plush teal velvet.

  • 🌊

    Romantic Canal-Side Terrace

    Gentle sea breezes, sparkling water reflections, and ambient twilight lantern lighting right at the canal's edge.

  • 👑

    Royal Maharaja Dining Hall

    Ornate brass chandeliers, hand-carved Mughal jali screens, green marble tables, and stylized Taj Mahal artwork.

  • 🥂

    Celebration & Family Alcoves

    Spacious plush booths tailored for intimate date nights, family get-togethers, and business entertaining.
